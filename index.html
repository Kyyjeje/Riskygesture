Gesture Synth Guide

Playing mode
Pitch shift

Move your left hand toward the top or bottom of the screen to shift the chord by one semitone.

Gesture Synth is an interactive musical instrument created by Eric Wei . Use your hands to create chords, melodies, and sounds in real-time!

Left Hand

Tilt
Inward → Major
Outward → Minor

Fingers (Scale Degree)
1 → I
2 → II
3 → III
4 → IV
5 → V
Index + Pinky → VI
Index + Pinky + Thumb → VII

Pitch Shift
Top 50% → Raise 1 semitone
Middle 30% → Normal
Bottom 20% → Lower 1 semitone

Enable Raise or Lower, then move your left hand toward the top or bottom of the screen to shift by one semitone.

Right Hand

Fingers (Chord Quality)
1 → Root Position
2 → 1st Inversion
3 → Major/Minor 7th
4 → Dominant/Diminished 7th

Octave
Thumb In → Higher Octave
Thumb Out → Lower Octave

Tilt
Inward → More Filter
Outward → Less Filter

Height
Higher → Louder
Lower → Softer
